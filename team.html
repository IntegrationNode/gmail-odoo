Questions

Before you start

Can I add or remove people?

Yes, whenever you want, from the portal. Raise or lower the seats, and give or take each one with the person’s email.

How is it billed?

By card, through Polar, which issues a single invoice for the whole team, monthly or yearly.

I already pay for Plus. What do I do?

Switch your Plus to Team without cancelling: the change applies right away and Polar credits what you haven’t used.

How do I share it with the team?

Invite each person by email and they activate their key in their Gmail. Step by step, in the User Guide.
