Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Overview

Odoo Integration for Gmail ("the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains how the Extension collects, uses, and protects your information.

Developer: OdooIntegrations Team

Information We Collect

Data Accessed from Gmail

The Extension accesses the following information from Gmail. Except where noted, only from the email you open:

  • Sender and recipient email addresses: Used to search for matching contacts in your Odoo instance
  • Email subject and body: Used when you choose to create opportunities, tasks, or tickets in Odoo
  • Email date and metadata: Used for logging emails to Odoo records
  • Your Gmail address: Read when Gmail loads, to check that a PLUS license belongs to this account, and to fill it in when you subscribe
  • Odoo notifications (PLUS): Only if you set up Odoo notifications: the Extension checks the sender of the emails in your Gmail lists and reads those your Odoo sends you, even if you don't open them, to label them by module
  • Attachments: Uploaded to your Odoo when you log an email or create a record from it

Data Accessed from Odoo

The Extension retrieves the following information from your Odoo instance:

  • Contact information (names, emails, phone numbers, companies)
  • CRM opportunities associated with contacts
  • Project tasks associated with contacts
  • Helpdesk tickets associated with contacts
  • Company information and related contacts
  • Quotations, sales orders, financial figures and the messages the contact appears in

Data Accessed for Automatic Logging (Optional)

Automatic logging is an opt-in feature. The Extension only accesses this data after you grant Gmail access in Settings > Email logging ("Connect Gmail") and explicitly link a thread to an Odoo record. Once a thread is linked, the Extension uses Google's Gmail API to:

  • Periodically check the identifiers of the new messages in your account to find those in the threads you have linked (only while at least one thread is linked)
  • Read the subject, sender, recipients, date and body of the new messages in those threads
  • Post them as internal notes in the Odoo records you linked

Scope: The Extension only requests the gmail.readonly OAuth scope. It cannot send, modify, archive or delete emails. Automatic logging stops when you turn it off for a thread or for all of them, or remove the Extension. Tokens can also be revoked from your Google Account permissions at any time.

Configuration Data

The Extension stores your configuration in Chrome's storage, in your browser:

  • Odoo instance URL
  • Database name
  • Authentication credentials
  • User preferences (visible sections settings)
  • Gmail OAuth tokens (only if you connect Gmail for automatic logging) — required to access the Gmail API on your behalf; they stay until you remove the Extension or revoke access from your Google Account
  • Automatic logging links (thread-to-record links and IDs of messages already logged) — stored in your browser while each link exists
  • Active company selection and the list of companies you have access to (multi-company instances) — cached locally to remember which company you are operating in

Open Odoo tabs

When you click "Autodetect Odoo", the extension reads the addresses of the odoo.com tabs open in this browser, only to offer you your instance. It does not read their content, and the addresses are neither stored nor sent.

How We Use Your Information

The Extension uses your information solely for the following purposes:

  • Display Contact Information: Show relevant Odoo contact details in Gmail's sidebar
  • Create Odoo Records: When you click "Create" buttons, the email content is sent to your Odoo instance to create opportunities, tasks, or tickets
  • Log Emails: When you click "Log Email" buttons, the email content is saved to your Odoo instance's chatter
  • Search and Filter: Search through opportunities, tasks, and tickets associated with contacts
  • Caching: Temporarily cache Odoo API responses locally to improve performance
  • Automatic Logging: When you turn on automatic logging for a record, the Extension checks your Gmail account in the background for new emails on the linked thread and posts them as internal notes in your Odoo instance
  • Odoo Notifications (PLUS): Label the emails your Odoo sends you by module and show where you were mentioned
  • Usage Statistics and Error Reports: Understand how the Extension is used and fix faults (see Analytics)
  • Subscription and Price: Manage your PLUS subscription, check that the license belongs to your Gmail account, and show you the price for your country (see Third-Party Services)
  • Odoo Autodetection: When you click "Autodetect Odoo", offer you your instance from your open odoo.com tabs

Data Storage and Security

Storage: Configuration and cache are stored in Chrome's storage, in your browser. If you use Chrome sync, Chrome copies your Odoo address, your API key and your preferences to your Google account and your other devices.

Data Transmission: The Extension communicates directly with YOUR Odoo instance using HTTPS. Your Odoo credentials are never sent to the extension developer.

Cache Management: Cached data stops being used after a few minutes and is deleted when you click the refresh button or connect to another Odoo. Removing the Extension deletes it from this browser.

Third-Party Services

Polar (Payment Processing)

The Extension uses Polar (https://polar.sh/) for subscription management and payment processing. When you subscribe to the PLUS plan, Polar collects:

  • Your email address for account management and receipts
  • Payment information (processed securely by Stripe, not stored by the Extension)
  • Subscription status and billing information
  • The installation ID, when you activate a license, to tell your devices apart

Polar Privacy Policy: https://polar.sh/legal/privacy

Important: Payment data is handled entirely by Polar and Stripe. The Extension never receives or stores your credit card information.

Price by Country

To show you the price for your country, the Extension asks ParityDeals (https://www.paritydeals.com/), which infers the country from your connection. It receives nothing from the Extension other than the request itself.

InboxSDK (Gmail Integration)

The Extension uses InboxSDK (https://www.inboxsdk.com/) to integrate with Gmail's interface: to display the sidebar and detect the contacts of the email you open.

PostHog (Analytics)

The Extension uses PostHog (https://posthog.com/) for usage analytics. PostHog helps us understand how the extension is used and identify issues. Data collected includes:

  • Installation ID (a random code). If you activate PLUS, it is associated with your license.
  • Feature usage events (e.g., "email logged", "record created")
  • Error reports for debugging, without email content (see Crash reports below)
  • A boolean flag indicating whether your Odoo user has access to more than one company (used to know whether multi-company features are relevant). The list of companies and their names are NOT sent.

PostHog Privacy Policy: https://posthog.com/privacy

Opt-out: You can disable usage analytics at any time in Settings > General ("Share anonymous usage data"). That setting covers most usage statistics; crash reports (described below) and a few service events, such as a change of plan, are sent regardless.

Crash reports: Crash reports are sent regardless of the analytics setting, because they describe the state of the application rather than your activity. Each report contains the error message and its technical details, the versions of the Extension and of Odoo, and the random installation ID — never email content or credentials. If the error comes from your Odoo, the report includes Odoo's error message, which may contain the address of your instance. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in detecting and fixing faults. If you have or had PLUS, you can object at any time by writing from your license email to odoointegrations.team@gmail.com.

Third-Party Data Sharing

We do NOT sell your data or share it for advertising or data brokering purposes.

The Extension and its services communicate with:

  • Your Odoo Instance: The Extension sends API requests to the Odoo instance URL you configure to retrieve and create records
  • Polar: Only for subscription management (email, installation ID, subscription status, payment processing)
  • PostHog: Usage statistics and error reports, without email content. Most usage statistics can be disabled in Settings > General ("Share anonymous usage data"); see Opt-out.
  • ParityDeals: Only to show you the price for your country
  • Gmail API (Google): Only if you turn on automatic logging
  • Google Forms: The uninstall survey, only if you submit it
  • GitHub: Internal notices about PLUS subscriptions with problems, with no email or name

PostHog stores the analytics in the European Union (Frankfurt). Others, such as GitHub, may process data outside it.

The Extension does NOT:

  • Sell your data to data brokers
  • Share data for advertising purposes
  • Send the content of your emails or your Odoo records to the extension developer

Google API Services User Data Policy

Odoo Integration for Gmail's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Limited Use Compliance:

  • The Extension only reads your emails when (a) you open an email, (b) you have set up Odoo notifications (PLUS), to read the emails your Odoo sends you, or (c) you have turned on automatic logging and linked a thread to an Odoo record (a per-thread opt-in)
  • Data read through the Gmail API is used exclusively for the disclosed purpose: logging those emails into your Odoo
  • No Gmail data is used for serving advertisements
  • Gmail data read through the Gmail API is only sent to your own Odoo instance
  • Gmail data is not used for determining creditworthiness or lending purposes
  • The Extension only requests the gmail.readonly OAuth scope. It cannot send, modify, archive or delete emails on your behalf

Permissions Explained

The Extension requests the following Chrome permissions:

Required Permissions (Granted at Installation)

  • scripting: Required to inject the sidebar interface into Gmail
  • storage: Required to save your Odoo configuration and cache API responses in Chrome's storage
  • host_permissions (mail.google.com): Required to access Gmail's interface and display the sidebar
  • host_permissions (*.odoo.com): Required to communicate with Odoo Online cloud instances (e.g., yourcompany.odoo.com) and, when you click "Autodetect Odoo", to read the addresses of your open odoo.com tabs
  • host_permissions (api.polar.sh): Required for subscription management and payment processing
  • host_permissions (api.paritydeals.com): Required to offer Purchasing Power Parity (PPP) discounts based on the user's country

Optional Permissions (Requested When Needed)

The Extension requests optional permissions for self-hosted Odoo instances and for automatic logging:

  • optional_host_permissions (https://*/*): Used ONLY if you connect to a self-hosted Odoo instance on a custom domain (e.g., odoo.yourcompany.com or erp.example.com), or for Google's API domain (www.googleapis.com) when you connect Gmail for automatic logging. Each address is requested only when you need it.
  • identity: Used ONLY when you opt into automatic logging. Lets the Extension complete the Google sign-in flow that grants Gmail API access (gmail.readonly scope). Requested when you turn on automatic logging or click "Connect Gmail" in Settings > Email logging.
  • alarms: Used ONLY when automatic logging is active. Lets the Extension wake up periodically in the background to check for new emails on the threads you have linked. Requested when you turn on automatic logging or click "Connect Gmail".

Note: The Extension only supports HTTPS connections for security. HTTP connections are not allowed.

When Optional Permissions Are Requested

Chrome will prompt you to grant optional permissions when:

  • You configure a custom Odoo URL that is not an *.odoo.com domain
  • You explicitly choose to connect to a self-hosted Odoo instance
  • You connect Gmail for automatic logging

You can deny these permissions if you only use Odoo Online (*.odoo.com). The Extension will work normally with Odoo Online without granting optional permissions, except automatic logging.

Why These Optional Permissions?

Many organizations host Odoo on their own servers with custom domains (e.g., erp.acme.com, odoo.mycompany.net). To support these users, the Extension requests optional broad permissions. However:

  • These permissions are ONLY used to communicate with YOUR configured Odoo instance and, with automatic logging, with Google's Gmail API
  • They are NEVER used to access other websites
  • You can revoke them at any time in Chrome settings
  • The Extension only requests them if you enter a custom Odoo URL or connect Gmail for automatic logging

Your Data Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: Your configuration is stored in your browser and your records in your Odoo, which you control. For your usage data, see Deletion.
  • Deletion: You can delete the Extension's data by:
    • Clicking the refresh button (clears cache)
    • Removing the Extension from Chrome (clears the data stored in this browser)
    • Asking us to delete your usage data: if you have or had PLUS, write to odoointegrations.team@gmail.com from your license email and we will delete the usage data of the installations linked to your license. If you never had PLUS, your usage data is not linked to your name or your email; we can only look for it if you give us your Odoo address and an error report contains it.
  • Portability: The records you create and the emails you log stay in your Odoo, and you can export them with Odoo's own tools
  • Control: You can stop the Extension at any time by removing it. If you connected Gmail, you can also revoke its access in your Google Account permissions.

Data Retention

The Extension retains data as follows:

  • Configuration Data: Stored in this browser until you remove the Extension
  • Cached API Responses: Stop being used after a few minutes; deleted when you click refresh or connect to another Odoo
  • User Preferences: Stored in this browser until you remove the Extension

When you uninstall the Extension, Chrome removes the data stored in this browser. If you use Chrome sync, your Odoo address, your API key and your preferences can stay in your Google account.

Children's Privacy

The Extension is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy and, if required, through the Extension's update notifications.

Continued use of the Extension after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated privacy policy.
